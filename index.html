OBSERVE

Movement tells us where to look.

Animation can establish hierarchy before a visitor reads a single word.

When an element enters the viewport, movement can signal that something new has arrived. Used carefully, this helps organize a long scrolling story rather than simply decorating it.

DEPTH

Scroll can create an illusion of space.

Elements moving at different speeds appear to exist at different distances.

Parallax is most effective when it is subtle. The movement should support the spatial composition rather than make reading difficult.
